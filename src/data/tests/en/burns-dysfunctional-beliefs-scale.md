Dysfunctional Attitude Scale (DAS) is a psychological questionnaire developed by David Burns to identify and measure the strength of deep-seated negative beliefs or "dysfunctional attitudes" — those that prevent a person from living life to the fullest and achieving their goals, and which are often associated with depressive states. It also lets you assess how much your sense of self-worth depends on the opinions of others, their love and approval.

**Instructions:** Choose the answer that best describes your attitude for each question.
